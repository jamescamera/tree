## Generation brief

Generate **one** adult female British Shorthair, seated upright and front-facing,
with a plush short coat. It must have visibly irregular **warm cinnamon** and
**pale cream** tortoiseshell patches (not stripes) on the face and forelegs, plus
a broad white bib, white muzzle, and white paws. Do not use blue, grey, lilac,
silver, black, or tabby markings. Keep the complete face, ears, whiskers, chest,
and paws in frame.

Use an opaque, 2048px-requested PNG with no cutout/transparency, no props/text,
and a perfectly flat saturated cyan background—no floor, gradient, horizon, or
shadow. Name it exactly as in the table and run the documented processor and QA.

Three generated attempts were rejected because they were solid cinnamon/white or
contained grey rather than a true cinnamon-and-cream tortie pattern. Until a
usable source exists, `fur.js` deliberately routes this phenotype to
`cinnamon.webp` and documents that visible fallback.
