    Where he comes from

    Cattery names carry their country with them — vom and von are German, van Het Hof Dutch, LV* is Latvia's FIFe prefix, a SVERAK registration is Swedish, and the UK side is written in GCCF breed numbers. Plot those and Yoshi turns out to be a thoroughly European cat.

    Why he's lilac

    Lilac isn't a colour a cat can inherit directly — it's two other things happening at once. The pedigree is enough to work out exactly what Yoshi must be carrying, and roughly where each piece came from.
